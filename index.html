My Celiac

Another thing about me that I don't usually share unless it's necessary, even though it impacts my life dramatically, is that I have Celiac Disease. This is an autoimmune disease that targets the small intestine. Basically, whenever I ingest gluten, even a tiny bit, my immune system thinks it's something bad for me and it attacks my body trying to get rid of it. I have been living with my diagnosis and been on a gluten free diet for 11 years now, but no matter how long it's been I can still get contaminated and wind up sick for a week. The physical symptoms are not the only ones though, but the mental side effects don't get talked about often. However, the month of May was Celiac awareness month and I was able to feel a sense of community with people sharing their stories online.
